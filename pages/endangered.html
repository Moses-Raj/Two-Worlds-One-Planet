Endangered Species 🐾

Discover the animals that need our protection before they disappear.

Marine Species 🌊

Hawksbill Turtle 🐢

⚠️ Critically Endangered

Habitat:
Tropical oceans, coral reefs, and coastal areas.

Threats:
• Plastic pollution
• Illegal wildlife trade
• Loss of nesting beaches

Protection:
• Marine protected areas
• Turtle conservation programs

Dugong 🌊

⚠️ Vulnerable

Habitat:
Coastal waters and seagrass ecosystems.

Threats:
• Loss of seagrass habitats
• Boat collisions
• Human disturbance

Protection:
• Marine conservation areas
• Protection of seagrass ecosystems

Whale Shark 🦈

⚠️ Endangered

Habitat:
Warm tropical oceans around the world.

Threats:
• Fishing activities
• Ocean pollution
• Habitat changes

Protection:
• Marine protected areas
• Sustainable fishing practices

How Can You Help Protect These Species? 🌱

🌊 Protect Marine Life

Reduce plastic waste, avoid littering, and support efforts that protect oceans and marine habitats.

🌳 Protect Forests

Support conservation efforts, protect forests, and respect wildlife habitats.

🐾 Protect Wildlife

Never support illegal wildlife trade and help spread awareness about endangered species.
