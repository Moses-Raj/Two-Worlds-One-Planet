SDG 15: Life on Land 🌳

Protect, restore, and promote sustainable use of terrestrial ecosystems and biodiversity.

Why Land Ecosystems Matter

Forests, grasslands, and wildlife habitats support life on Earth. They provide clean air, protect climate, and maintain biodiversity.

Threats To Life On Land ⚠️

Deforestation

Forest destruction causes habitat loss and threatens wildlife survival.

Loss Of Biodiversity

Many species are disappearing due to human activities and environmental changes.

Land Degradation

Pollution and unsustainable farming damage the quality of land ecosystems.

How We Can Help ✅

Plant trees, protect wildlife, reduce waste, and support sustainable land management.
